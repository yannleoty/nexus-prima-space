Nexus Prima Space

For Earth And Beyond.

01Mission

Nexus Prima Space is a space technology company dedicated to developing autonomous robotics, extraterrestrial resource production and the industrial infrastructure required for a sustainable interplanetary civilisation.

Our ambition is to make essential infrastructure increasingly autonomous, economically viable and capable of operating beyond Earth.

Beginning with technologies applicable to industrial operations on Earth, we aim to progressively develop the capabilities required to construct, operate and sustain infrastructure on the Moon, asteroids and Mars.

Our long-term objective is to help humanity build an independent, productive and sustainable economy beyond Earth.

02Phases

PHASE 01 · Autonomous Robot Systems

Develop robotic systems for useful industrial work with an emphasis on coordinated and resilient autonomous operations.

PHASE 02 · Extraterrestrial Resource Production

Progress toward technologies extracting and processing resources beyond Earth.

PHASE 03 · Space Industrial Infrastructure

Advance, build, operate and maintain productive infrastructure on the Moon, asteroids and Mars.

03Product

ARS1

“ARS1” for “Autonomous Robot Systems - Product 1” is our first prototype product.

ARS1 is being developed as autonomous technology systems that inspect and provide maintenance of solar-power and industrial infrastructure to reduce the cost, complexity and risk of operating infrastructure in extreme environments.
