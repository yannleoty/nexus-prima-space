01 / Purpose

Our mission.

AUTONOMY · INDUSTRY · SPACE

Nexus Prima Space is a proposed space technology company dedicated to developing autonomous robotics, extraterrestrial resource production and the industrial infrastructure required for a sustainable interplanetary civilisation.

Our ambition is to make essential infrastructure increasingly autonomous, economically viable and capable of operating beyond Earth.

Beginning with technologies applicable to industrial operations on Earth, we aim to progressively develop the capabilities required to construct, operate and sustain infrastructure on the Moon, asteroids and Mars.

Our long-term objective is to help humanity build an independent, productive and sustainable economy beyond Earth.

Concept / Initial focus

AR1

A proposed coordinated robotic workforce for demanding environments: surveying terrain, preparing sites, constructing and maintaining infrastructure.

The intended starting point is practical terrestrial industrial applications. The longer-term ambition is to adapt and extend these capabilities to infrastructure beyond Earth.

03 / Long-term development strategy

Three phases.
One direction.

PHASE 01 · INITIAL FOCUS

Autonomous Robots

Develop and validate robotic systems for useful industrial work on Earth, with an emphasis on coordinated, resilient autonomous operations.

PHASE 02 · LONG-TERM

Extraterrestrial Resource Production

Progress toward technologies for identifying, extracting and processing resources beyond Earth to support sustained activity.

PHASE 03 · LONG-TERM

Space Industrial Infrastructure

Advance the systems needed to build, operate and maintain productive infrastructure on the Moon, asteroids and Mars.

04 / Long-term vision

A future in which humanity can build and sustain productive infrastructure beyond its home planet.

The path begins with solving real problems on Earth. Our approach is to pursue useful technologies, build capabilities progressively and maintain a long-term perspective.
